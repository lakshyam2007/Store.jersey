About RIVAL

Kits made for the people in the stands.

We started RIVAL because match-day jerseys were built for TV cameras, not for supporters standing in the rain for 90 minutes. Every kit we make is tested by the people who actually wear them.

Our story

Since 2019

Built by supporters, for supporters.

RIVAL was founded by three season-ticket holders who were tired of official kits that shrank after two washes. We partner directly with clubs and fan groups to design jerseys that fit real bodies, hold their colour, and survive a full season of home and away trips.
