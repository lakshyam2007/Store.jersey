FAQ

Common questions

How do RIVAL jerseys fit?

Regular athletic fit — true to size for most supporters. If you're between sizes or prefer a looser fit for layering, size up. Check the size guide on each product page for shoulder-to-hem measurements.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days; expedited options are available at checkout. Orders over $75 ship free.

Can I return or exchange a jersey?

Yes — unworn jerseys with tags attached can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery. Start a return from your order confirmation email or by contacting us directly.

Do you make custom club kits?

We partner directly with supporters' clubs and amateur teams on custom colourways and crests. Reach out through this contact form with "Custom kit" in the subject line and we'll follow up.
